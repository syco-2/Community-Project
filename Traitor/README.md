# Traitor

One phone, one village, one lie. The group sits in a circle, the phone goes from hand to hand
and each player reads a secret role. The phone narrates the night, the day and the vote out
loud, in English or German.

## A round

- Setup: names, wolves, cupid and the hunter on or off, then the deal.
- Deal: every player takes the phone, opens a card and passes it on.
- Night: the cupid links two players, the wolves pick, the seer looks, the witch heals or poisons.
- Day: the phone reads who died and how, then the group talks.
- Vote: every living player votes on the same phone, one after another, in secret. Most votes leave, a tie leaves nobody.
- Hunter: when the hunter is out, one shot goes with them.
- Result: winner, all roles, score, next round.
