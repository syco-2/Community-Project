# App Icon Package

Production-ready app icons for the Apple App Store and/or Google Play Store,
built from your source image. Sourced against Apple's current Human
Interface Guidelines and the official Android adaptive/themed icon
specification.

## Android (`android/res/`)

- **Legacy launcher icons** — `mipmap-{mdpi,hdpi,xhdpi,xxhdpi,xxxhdpi}/ic_launcher.png`, sized from the 48dp baseline. Used as the fallback icon on API < 26 and by launchers that don't support adaptive icons.
- **Adaptive icon** (API 26+) — foreground, background, and monochrome (themed icon) layers, each on the 108dp canvas with your artwork kept inside the 66dp safe-zone circle so it isn't clipped by circular, squircle, or rounded-square launcher masks. `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml` and `ic_launcher_round.xml` reference the layers.
- **Play Store listing icon** — `play-store-icon.png`, 512×512px, uploaded separately in Play Console (never bundled into the APK/AAB). Google's Play Store listing preview now applies a **30% corner radius** (raised from 20% in March 2026) — this only affects the listing thumbnail, not your launcher or adaptive icon, but keep important detail away from the corners.
- **Monochrome (themed icon) layer included.** As of the Android 16 QPR2 Play policy update, Google Play requires developers to let users theme their app's icon — apps without a monochrome layer now get one auto-generated by a color filter, which often looks worse than a hand-tuned version. This package includes one.

### Install

1. Copy the `android/res/mipmap-*` folders into your project's `app/src/main/res/`, replacing the existing `ic_launcher*` files.
2. Copy `mipmap-anydpi-v26/` in as well — this is what activates the adaptive icon on Android 8.0+.
3. Upload `play-store-icon.png` directly in Play Console → App content → Store settings, not as part of the app bundle.

## Notes

- Design your source artwork with essential detail away from the outer edge — both platforms reserve a margin (Android's adaptive safe zone, Apple's system mask, Play Store's listing corner radius) that may crop content near the edges.
- A 1024×1024px (or larger) source with a simple, high-contrast mark produces the sharpest result across every generated size.
